# ProfitPocket

**Bookkeeping & inventory for small traders.**

ProfitPocket helps small traders and retail business owners track their business without needing an accounting background.

🔗 **Live site:** https://profit-pocket-tviz.vercel.app

![ProfitPocket dashboard](screenshot.png)

## Features

- **Products:** add products with cost and selling prices
- **Sales:** log sales as they happen; stock levels update automatically
- **Expenses:** record business expenses
- **Real profit:** calculates profit after cost of goods sold
- **Dashboard:** at-a-glance view of products, sales, expenses and net profit
- **Reports:** profit and loss by day, week, month or year
